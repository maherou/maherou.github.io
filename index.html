I'm Mike Heroux. After retiring from full-time roles at Sandia National Laboratories and teaching at St. John's University, I now consult and advise on scientific and high-performance computing software strategy — helping organizations build, sustain, and get value from the software ecosystems that power HPC and AI-driven research.
