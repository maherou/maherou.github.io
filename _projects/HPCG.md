The High-Performance Conjugate Gradients (HPCG) benchmark is part of the TOP500 computer ranking system, complementing LINPACK. I am the designer and developer of the reference code. HPCG was a 2019 R&D 100 Honorable Mention project.

The two key reports that describe HPCG are:
- [Toward a New Metric for Ranking High Performance Computing Systems]({{ '/Projects/files/docs/HPCG-Benchmark.pdf' | relative_url }})
- [HPCG Technical Specification]({{ '/Projects/files/docs/HPCG-Specification.pdf' | relative_url }})

GitHub: [https://github.com/hpcg-benchmark](https://github.com/hpcg-benchmark). Website: [https://hpcg-benchmark.org](https://hpcg-benchmark.org).
