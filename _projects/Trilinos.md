The Trilinos project is a community effort to develop scientific and mathematical libraries that enable the solution of large-scale multi-physics, multi-scale and related modeling and simulation problems on leadership computing platforms. I have led the project since its inception in 2000. Trilinos was a 2004 R&D 100 Award winner.

GitHub: [https://github.com/trilinos/Trilinos](https://github.com/trilinos/Trilinos). Website: [https://trilinos.github.io](https://trilinos.github.io).
