Since retiring from full-time roles at Sandia National Laboratories (2024) and teaching at
St. John's University (2026), my work is advisory, part-time, and by design: a handful of
sustained engagements rather than a single employer. If you're weighing a question in one of
the areas below, I'm glad to talk.

## Where I can help

- **HPC-AI software strategy** &mdash; assessing where an organization's scientific or AI
  computing software stack is heading, and what investments actually move the needle.
- **Software ecosystem stewardship** &mdash; the kind of curation and sustainability work behind
  E4S, Trilinos, and the Better Scientific Software project: how to keep a portfolio of
  interdependent software healthy over years, not just at release time.
- **Research software practices** &mdash; productivity, sustainability, and reproducibility
  practices for scientific software teams, informed by the IDEAS and Research Software Science
  projects.
- **Advisory and board input** &mdash; drawing on past service on the NSF Advisory Committee for
  Cyberinfrastructure and the Molecular Sciences Software Institute Advisory Board.
- **Speaking and writing** &mdash; on HPC-AI software strategy, scientific software
  sustainability, and generative AI in technical education and research; see
  [Writing]({{ '/Writing/' | relative_url }}).
